what happens next

  1. We read your message — a real person, usually within a few days.
  2. We reply and point you at whatever's coming up nearest you.
  3. We point you to the WhatsApp group chat for your city, so there are people there between events too.
  4. You come to something. Or you don't, and we don't chase you.
We won't pass your details to anyone, and we won't add you to anything you didn't ask for.
