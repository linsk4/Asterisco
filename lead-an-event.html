The idea

you're in the lead — we handle the rest

Any woman with an idea for an event can bring it to Asterisco*: a sport you want to introduce, a topic you want to raise, an experience you want to create.

It's a co-creation process. We help you find partners, a venue and the practical pieces you need. You take care of the content, the promotion and the delivery. But you're the one deciding, organising and owning the outcome.

For many of the women who've done it, this was their first real experience of leadership and responsibility. And consistently — from the women who led, and from us — it's described as the most empowering part of the whole thing.

How it works

four steps, start to finish

01

you bring the idea

Tell us what you'd like to do and roughly where. It can be one sentence. It does not need to be finished, polished or realistic yet.

02

we work it out together

We sit down with you and turn it into a plan: what's needed, what's possible, what to cut. We find the venue, the partners and the equipment.

03

you lead it

On the day, it's yours. You run the session, welcome people and set the tone. We're there beside you, not in front of you.

04

it keeps going

The group stays in touch, and often keeps meeting on its own. Several one-off events have turned into ongoing series exactly this way.

Who does what

so you know exactly what you're taking on

*

you own

  • The idea, and what the event is actually about
  • The content — what happens, in what order
  • Welcoming people and setting the tone on the day
  • Helping promote it to the people you want there
  • The final say on every decision
*

we handle

  • Finding a venue, a club or a partner willing to host
  • Equipment, and negotiating access or discounts
  • Promotion through our channels and local groups
  • Sign-ups, logistics and the admin around it all
  • Being there on the day so you're not doing it alone

It has already worked

one workshop became a series of rides

In Turin, a woman in the community knew how to repair bikes. She wanted others to feel confident enough to head out on long rides — even alone.

We found the bike shop, handled the logistics and got the word out. She ran the workshop. What started as one afternoon of fixing punctures turned into an ongoing series of group rides that's still going.

She had never led an event before.

Questions

the things people ask first

do i need to be an expert?

No. You need to know one thing well enough to share it — and “well enough” is a much lower bar than you think. The women who've led our events weren't coaches or professional athletes. One knew how to fix a bike. One knew about nutrition. That was enough.

i've never organised anything before

Good — that's exactly who this is for. Most of the women who've led an Asterisco* event had never done it before either. The organising is the part we help with most, precisely so that a lack of experience isn't what stops you.

does it have to be a sport?

No. Some of our best events pair movement with a conversation about something else entirely — food and the body, money and independence, the gender gap in the outdoors. If there's a topic you want to raise, we can build an activity around it.

what if i'm not in one of your cities?

Tell us anyway. We're active in Porto, Turin, Trondheim and Cagliari, and new local communities are forming all the time — often because one woman got in touch with an idea. Yours could be the reason we're somewhere new.

how much of my time will this take?

Less than you'd expect, because the admin isn't yours. Realistically: a couple of conversations with us, some time preparing your part, and the day itself. We shape the scale around what you can actually give.

does it cost me anything?

No. You don't fund the event and you don't take on financial risk. We work with venues, clubs and partners to keep costs low or covered, and we're honest with you upfront about what's possible.

what if my idea isn't good enough?

Send it. We've never had someone tell us an idea we thought was too small. The point of the umbrella is that you don't have to have it all worked out — that's the part we do together.
