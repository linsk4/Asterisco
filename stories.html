in our own words*

Not Asterisco* publishing content about women — women publishing their own stories and thinking, with us providing the platform.

What this space is for

beyond the polished “who we are”

This is where the community's actual voices and thinking live. It's open to anyone in it.

Women who led an event — writing about organising and leading it: what they learned, what scared them, what it felt like to be in charge.

Women who took part — trying a sport for the first time, a friendship that started at an event, a moment of growth or confidence.

Reflections and debates raised at events — nutrition and the body, the gender gap in sport and leadership, body image, self-love and limits, women and nature.

Community-led research and environmental reflections, published as they develop rather than locked away in a report.
