## Mission statement (resolved)

The concept overview (p.7) flagged a mismatch between two mission lines and asked for
confirmation before briefing website copy:

- Concept Overview: "Connecting women through sports, movement and nature."
- Brand Guide v1: "Sport as a tool for empowerment, self-knowledge and leadership — by
  women, for women."

**Confirmed mission statement to use for the website:**

> Sport as a tool for empowerment, leadership, and connection.

Use this wording (or close variations of it) wherever the site needs a mission
statement, rather than either line above.
