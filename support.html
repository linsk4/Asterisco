Partner & support

back the women who show up*

Every event we run depends on a venue, a club, an expert or a sponsor who decided this was worth backing. Here's how you can be one of them.

Why it matters

small support, disproportionate effect

We're not asking anyone to fund a stadium. A court for two hours, a workshop space on a Sunday morning, a set of borrowed rackets — that's usually the whole difference between an event happening and not happening.

Our costs are low by design, because the model is built on partnership rather than ownership. We don't run facilities or employ coaches. We connect women who already have knowledge with places willing to host them.

That's why modest support goes a long way here: 11 women played padel for the first time because a local club said yes to one weekend.

4 cities with active local communities

6+ sports so far: climbing, cycling, running, padel, hiking, skiing

Who we're looking for

two kinds of partnership

*

organizations

We're seeking collaborations with organizations registered in the EU that are active in the social sector and experienced in working with young people — grounded in horizontal exchange, mutual learning and collective strengthening.

If that's how your organization works, we're likely a good fit. We're interested in genuine exchange rather than being a line item in someone else's programme.

  • Joint projects and funding applications
  • Exchange of methods and facilitators
  • Shared research on women, sport and the outdoors
*

local partners & sponsors

Venues, sports clubs, experts, shops and brands who can host or support an individual event — in any of our cities, or in one where we're not yet active.

This is the most immediately useful kind of support, and the easiest to say yes to.

  • A venue, court, wall or workshop space for a few hours
  • Equipment to borrow, or a discount on hire
  • An expert willing to share what she knows
  • Sponsorship of a single event or a local series

What partners get

a real audience, not an impression count

We're a small, early-stage organization and we won't pretend otherwise. What we can offer is specific and honest.

direct reach

Women who actually turn up in person, in your city — not passive followers. Many are new to the sport and looking for where to go next.

visibility

Credit across our channels, on the event itself, on this site, and in the stories we publish afterwards.

a genuine story

Content and photography from a real community event — the kind that's hard to manufacture and easy to stand behind.

measurable impact

We tell you plainly how many women came, what they tried, and what happened afterwards.

Where we are right now

a registered ngo, and straightforward about it

Asterisco* is a formally registered non-governmental organization in Norway, with its administrative office in Trondheim.

That gives partners and funders a legal entity to work with: we can sign agreements, receive grants and hold funds in our own name. We also plan to enrol in Norway's public grassroots-funding scheme, Grasrotandelen.

We're still young, and still run by three women alongside their other commitments. Events have taken place in Porto, Turin and the Trondheim area, with active communities now in those cities and Cagliari.

We tell you this upfront because we'd rather partner with people who know exactly what they're backing: something real and growing, properly constituted, and early enough that your support genuinely shapes it.
